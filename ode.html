Getting Started with the Solver

Hi Math-ants! Enter your equation in the input box and press to get the result.

By default the solver expects an ODE. If you're solving a general math expression,
click to open the toolkit and switch to MATH mode first.

For initial value problems, separate your conditions with ;
For example: dy/dx = x ; y(0) = 1 then press .

Phase plots will soon be available to the right of the solution panel — stay tuned!
