I am AB, a follower of the way of truth. I am a scientist and mathematician with an Electrical Engineering Master's degree specialising in Energy Systems. I worked in a Power Electronics Research Company as a Research Scientist, where I designed and developed a novel Electrical Motor — the SynRM Motor. Driven by my passion for multidisciplinary research, I resigned to start my own Research Company.

Mathgabs is the first product of that passion. I envision it as a platform where I can share my knowledge and skills with the world in a way that is accessible to everyone. I am deeply enthusiastic about mathematics, and through philosophical observation I have come to understand that it is the language spoken and understood symbolically, only by us — humans — out of all the species observable in the Universe. The ability to communicate with the inanimate concepts and phenomena of the Universe is a creative power shared with us by the Creator, always within the grasp of our fingers.

You may think that we are mere ants relative to the vastness of the Universe — but I have a different perspective. Even a 2 mm ant can be described with an infinite number of digits when its length is mathematically derived, while at the same time the 93-billion-lightyear span of the observable Universe can be expressed in just two significant digits. This ability to shift perspective is what makes us human. We are not merely wandering ants — in my view, we are Math-ants , and this is our Ant-hill. I hope you have fun learning mathematics, and I wish that this Ant-hill guides you along the path of wisdom.
